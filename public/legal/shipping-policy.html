Shipping Policy

Order Processing

Please allow 1–3 business days for order processing before your gear ships. Drop days or high-volume releases may take 3–5 business days.

Domestic Shipping (US)

We offer standard and expedited shipping via USPS and UPS. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days.

International Shipping

We ship worldwide! International rates are calculated at checkout. Please note that buyers are responsible for any customs/duty fees applied by their destination country.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order leaves our Minneapolis facility, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number.
