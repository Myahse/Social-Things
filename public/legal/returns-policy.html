Return & Exchange Policy

Returns Window

We gladly accept returns within 30 days of your delivery date.

Condition

Items must be unwashed, unworn, and in the exact condition they were received, complete with all original tags.

Store Credit vs. Refund

You can choose to get a full refund to your original payment method (minus a return shipping fee) or receive 110% of the item value in Social Thing store credit.

Final Sale

Items marked as “Final Sale” cannot be returned or exchanged.

How to Initiate

To start a return, email us at returns@socialthing.com with your order number and we will guide you through the process.
